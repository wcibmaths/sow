Fix Year 12 / Year 13 unit-assessment folder links in index.html.
Do not change the SoW arrays, Firebase, or the auth gate. Do not refactor.

IAL assessment list, so every downstream call to assessmentFolderUrl() for
Year 12/13 receives unit=undefined and returns ''. The Y12_P12_ASSESSMENT_FOLDERS
URLs already exist and are correct — they are simply never reached.

=========================================================
In function ialAssessmentsFor(yg, set), add  unit:r.unit  to the two
out.push({...}) calls that represent real unit tests. Change:

  out.push({name:utName, sub:r.obj||'', week:r.week, dates:r.dates,
            term:r.term, kind:'eou', type:'Unit Test'});
to:
  out.push({name:utName, sub:r.obj||'', week:r.week, dates:r.dates,
            term:r.term, kind:'eou', type:'Unit Test', unit:r.unit});

past-paper block have no single unit folder, and they must keep returning no link.
